Paint Protection Film

PPF: Protection Built For Your Car

Paint Protection Film is a clear layer applied to your car’s paint to help guard against stone chips, minor scratches, and everyday wear. We inspect your vehicle first, then recommend the coverage and film grade that suits your car, your budget, and how you drive, explained beforehand.

Coating Options

Ceramic Or Graphene Coating For Cars

Ceramic and graphene coatings both add a protective layer over your car’s paint, improving gloss and making the surface easier to clean and maintain. Graphene is a newer option some manufacturers position as more durable than standard ceramic. We’ll explain the difference during your inspection, so you can choose the coating that fits your car and your budget.

Denting And Painting

Dent Repair And Painting For Cars

Our denting and painting service repairs dents and restores your car’s paint to a smooth, even finish. Every job starts with an inspection so we can match the paint accurately and explain the process before we begin. Whether it’s a small dent or a full respray, we’ll walk you through the options first.
